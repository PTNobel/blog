My mother taught me to make food when I was 19.  My grandmother taught me to cook a few years later.
My grandmother keeps no recipes, and has no written notes, I have taken notes on every time we have cooked together.

I want to share some of my those recipes here.

When my grandmother visits this one relative of ours, she has to bring three equally-sized jars of her lemon pickle (nimbu umpinkai in Kannada).
At first, she'd bring one jar and the husband and wife would play increasingly elaborate tricks on each other to try and keep it all for themselves. 
Then, she'd bring two jars: one for each.
Inevitably, they would start playing tricks on each other to try and shift the burden of whose jar was used to serve guests.
Finally, she started bringing three.

Here are my creations of that recipe.

## Tannegaye Nimbu Umpinkai

Wash lemons, leave out (not in fridge) to dry for at least 24 hours  
Chop lemons into small pieces  
Stir in salt; an ⅛ of volume of the lemon minimum  
Stir in ingu (asofetedia/hing) and arshna (turmeric) to lemons; a LOT of ingu  
Let sit  
Dry roast menthya (fenugreek) till brown; add sasve (mustard seeds) dry roast not too long, stop before burning  
Powder sasve and menthya  
Stir sasve menthya pudi into lemons  
Stir mensan kai pudi (red chilli powder) (we did 4 tablespoons for umpinkai that filled a jar that came with 42 oz of jam; we could have done more) into the lemons  
Taste, adjust salt and spices  
Fill jar, plastic wrap over top, close jar  
Let sit 2 to 3 days on countertop, shaking every day  
Put in fridge on third day  

## Bisi Nimbu Umpinkai

*Note: Ajji says you can do this with pineapple, mango, etc.*

Make sasve and menthya pudi from tannegaye nimbu umpinkai  
Roast sasve in yenai (oil); don’t pop  
Add arshna and ingu and stir a little  
Add lemon and stir  
Add uppu (salt); generous but not as much as the normal umpinkai  
Boil lemons in their juice  
Add mensan kai pudi and sasve menthya pudi  
Add bela (jaggery) or brown sucre (sugar)  
Stir  
Let cook  
Turn off heat, let sit uncovered till it completely cools. Can transfer to jars while cooling, but cannot cover jars.
